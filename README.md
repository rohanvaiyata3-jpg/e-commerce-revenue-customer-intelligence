# E-Commerce Revenue & Customer Intelligence

An end-to-end e-commerce analytics project using **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to understand revenue performance, customer behaviour, product performance, discounts, geography, and sales channels.

The project follows a practical analytics workflow - starting with raw transaction data, moving through exploration and structured SQL analysis, and ending with an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## What I wanted to understand

I used the dataset to answer business questions such as:

- How is revenue performing over time?
- Which categories and products drive the most revenue?
- How are customers behaving across orders and sales channels?
- What does repeat purchasing look like?
- How do discounts relate to sales performance?
- Which states contribute most to revenue?
- How do online and offline channels compare?
- Has business performance changed between 2024 and 2025?

The focus was not just on calculating metrics, but on finding patterns that could be useful for business decisions.
